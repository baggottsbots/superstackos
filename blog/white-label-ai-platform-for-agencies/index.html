White Label AI Platform for Agencies: How to Launch Your Own

By SuperStack OS Team•Sep 25, 2026•8 min read

Selling services by the hour caps your income. A white label AI platform lets your agency package the tools your clients already need, put your own brand on them and charge a monthly subscription that grows with each new account. Here is how the model works and what to look for before you commit to a vendor.

Why agencies are moving from services to a platform

Clients no longer want a pile of disconnected tools plus a retainer to keep them running. They want one system that handles leads, follow-ups, bookings, payments and reporting, and a partner who sets it up. When you resell a platform under your own name, you keep the relationship, the brand and the recurring revenue.

What to look for in a white label AI platform

  • Real white-labeling. Your domain, logo, colors, email sender and pricing, not a co-branded login.
  • Client sub-accounts. Separate workspaces so each client's contacts, agents and automations stay apart.
  • Breadth in one place. CRM, automation, email and SMS, voice, e-commerce, landing pages and content, so you are not reselling five vendors.
  • Snapshots. Clone a proven setup into a new client account in minutes.
  • AI agents that act. Agents with custom functions, not just a chat widget.
  • MCP connectivity. A branded white label MCP connector so clients can run their business from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok.

How to price your branded platform

There is no single right number, but most agencies use one of three structures:

  1. Setup fee plus monthly subscription. Charge once for configuration and monthly for platform access and support.
  2. Tiered plans. Mirror a Basic, Pro and Premium ladder so clients can start small and upgrade.
  3. Managed service bundle. Include ongoing campaign work and optimization in a higher monthly price.

Work out your cost per client first, including platform fees, voice minutes and your own time, then set a price that leaves a healthy margin.

Onboard clients faster with snapshots

Build one strong template account: your lead capture forms, follow-up automations, pipeline stages, booking flow and an AI agent tuned for your niche. Save it as a snapshot, then clone it for every new client and only change the branding and details. What used to take a week becomes an afternoon.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Reselling before you have tested the platform on your own business.
  • Pricing below your real cost once voice, SMS and support are included.
  • Skipping onboarding. The clients who succeed are the ones you walk through the first campaign.
  • Building a different setup for every client instead of one reusable snapshot.

Tip: pick a single niche, such as dental practices or fitness studios, and perfect one snapshot for it before you widen your offer.
