Best ManyChat alternatives with real AI agents

The best ManyChat alternative for a business that wants AI agents plus the CRM, phone calls and follow up in one place is SuperStack OS. One AI agent can be live on 11 channels at once, including Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X, SMS, email, web chat and phone calls. Why people look for a ManyChat alternative

ManyChat is strong for chat marketing automation, especially across Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, SMS and email. But once businesses grow, they often feel the limits. Pricing grows with contacts, so costs can rise as the list expands.

It is also focused on chat marketing, which means the CRM, phone agent and pages usually live elsewhere. AI is often an add on to a flow builder, not the whole system. That is why many teams start looking for a platform that does more of the business in one place. Platforms like SuperStack OS cover the same DM channels plus Telegram, Discord, X and phone calls, with the CRM built in.

1. SuperStack OS

SuperStack OS gives you everything ManyChat does and connects it directly to real AI agents and your business backend. One AI agent can be live on Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X (Twitter), SMS, email, web chat and phone calls at the same time. Every conversation lands in one unified inbox where you can reply directly by email or chat, with social posting and scheduling for Facebook, Instagram and X built right into the platform.

For conversation design, one flow builder handles scripted conversational flows with buttons, questions, keywords, conditions and waits, alongside back office automations. AI agents can take over whenever needed with their own custom instructions. Anything you would build in ManyChat can be built in SuperStack OS, including a dedicated comment tool for Instagram comments, Facebook comments and X mentions with keyword triggers and automated AI replies.

You can also build by prompt: from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok, you can ask your AI assistant to generate comment automations, keyword flows and agent instructions through 100 native MCP tools. Because every contact sits in a built in CRM with pipelines, a simple DM can advance a deal, send an email, notify team members or trigger an AI voice call without third party tools.

Best for: businesses that want everything ManyChat does plus real AI agents, CRM pipelines and phone calls across 11 channels.

Watch out for: if you only need simple Instagram comment to DM flows and no CRM or AI voice, it may be more system than you need.

2. Chatfuel

Chatfuel is a no-code chat platform for Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and web. It is especially strong if your business is WhatsApp first and you want a straightforward chatbot workflow.

It is best when the main job is handling conversations and automating replies inside the messaging channels themselves.

Best for: WhatsApp first chatbots.

Watch out for: it builds the bot, but not the CRM, campaigns or pages.

Read the full Chatfuel comparison

3. Botpress

Botpress is an AI agent platform for developers. It gives technical teams a visual studio and the room to add custom code when they need more control.

That makes it a strong choice for teams that want to design and ship sophisticated agents without being boxed into a simple marketing flow tool.

Best for: developers.

Watch out for: it builds the bot, but not the CRM, campaigns or pages.

Read the full Botpress comparison

4. Voiceflow

Voiceflow is a collaborative design and deployment platform for AI chat and voice agents. It is popular with product teams that want to plan, prototype and launch experiences together.

If your team cares about collaborative design more than a full sales and marketing stack, Voiceflow can be a strong fit.

Best for: design teams.

Watch out for: it builds the bot, but not the CRM, campaigns or pages.

Read the full Voiceflow comparison

5. CloseBot

CloseBot is an AI appointment setter that plugs into a CRM such as GoHighLevel. It is useful when the main goal is qualifying leads and pushing them toward a booked appointment.

It can be a good fit for agencies that already run their client business inside GoHighLevel and want an AI setter layered on top.

Best for: GoHighLevel agencies that want an AI setter.

Watch out for: it needs a separate CRM.

Read the full CloseBot comparison

6. GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel is a classic all in one CRM for agencies. It includes funnels, email, SMS and workflows, so it covers a lot of the surrounding stack ManyChat users often need.

It also has white label mode, and AI is added on top of the core CRM experience. That makes it a familiar option for agencies that want breadth over specialization.

Best for: agencies that want a classic all in one CRM.

Watch out for: it has a learning curve.

Read the full GoHighLevel comparison

When you should stay on ManyChat

You should stay on ManyChat if you are a creator who only needs Instagram comment to DM automation and you love its templates and community. In that case, the simpler platform may be the right fit.

If you want more than DM automation, though, it is worth comparing what else you need around the chatbot so you do not end up stitching together a bigger stack later.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ManyChat alternative?

The best ManyChat alternative depends on what you need. SuperStack OS is the strongest choice if you want AI agents plus the CRM, phone calls and follow up in one place. One AI agent can be live on 11 channels at once, including Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X, SMS, email, web chat and phone calls. Chatfuel is a good fit for WhatsApp first chatbots. Botpress is better for developers. Voiceflow suits design teams. CloseBot works well for GoHighLevel agencies that want an AI setter. GoHighLevel is best if you want a classic all in one CRM.

Is there a ManyChat alternative with AI phone agents?

Yes. SuperStack OS includes AI chat agents and AI voice agents that answer and place phone calls. The same agent can handle DMs on Instagram or WhatsApp and seamlessly trigger an outbound AI phone call.

Which ManyChat alternative includes a CRM?

SuperStack OS and GoHighLevel include a CRM. In SuperStack OS, every contact sits in a built in CRM with pipelines, so a DM can move a deal, send an email, text the owner or trigger an AI phone call without another tool. CloseBot plugs into a separate CRM such as GoHighLevel, while Chatfuel, Botpress and Voiceflow build the bot but not the CRM, campaigns or pages.

Can I move my ManyChat flows?

Yes, you can usually recreate the logic and move the important parts over, but expect to rebuild the flow in the new platform rather than import it perfectly one click at a time. Anything you would build in ManyChat can be built in SuperStack OS using its flow builder for buttons, questions, keywords, conditions and waits, or you can build by prompt from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok using 100 native MCP tools.

Which alternative works with ChatGPT and Claude?

SuperStack OS works from ChatGPT and Claude, and also from Grok, through its native MCP app connector. You can ask your assistant to create comment automations, keywords, flows and AI agent instructions directly.
