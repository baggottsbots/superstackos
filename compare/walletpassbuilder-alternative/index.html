Wallet passes and customer loyalty

SuperStack OS vs WalletPassBuilder: wallet passes without the meter

By SuperStack OS Team•Last verified November 2026

The short answer

WalletPassBuilder is a focused no-code pass creator for Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with a $49/month subscription that includes 1,000 pass credits. SuperStack OS has wallet passes built into a complete AI business operating system at the same $49 starting price. Loyalty passes, CRM, agents, pages, campaigns and payments live together, and you can run them from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok.

One piece of the puzzle vs the whole puzzle

The same $49 a month buys two different things. WalletPassBuilder gives you wallet passes and a credit meter: 1,000 pass credits included, with extra packs available as you grow. SuperStack OS gives you wallet passes plus the CRM, AI chat and voice agents, landing pages, email, SMS and payments.

A wallet push notification is the only way WalletPassBuilder can message your customer. SuperStack OS can also text, email, DM and call them. The loyalty pass sits on the same contact record as the conversations, campaigns and payments, rather than becoming another isolated customer touchpoint.

SuperStack OS brings the surrounding business tools together:

  • Apple Wallet and Google Wallet points programs and punch cards, wallet push notifications and location based alerts, with no app for the customer to download
  • AI chat and voice agents on Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X, SMS, email, web chat and phone, with one unified inbox and lead qualification and booking
  • Built-in CRM with pipelines, tags and custom fields
  • Automation flows and conversational flows in one builder
  • An AI Coding Agent that builds full multi-page websites, landing pages, interactive quizzes and games, plus forms and a web shop with Stripe checkout
  • Email and SMS broadcasts, post scheduling for Facebook, Instagram and X, and comment automation for Instagram comments, Facebook comments and X mentions, with keywords and AI replies
  • AI content generation for images, video, voice and music
  • A white label option so agencies can sell it as their own platform

A fair note on fit. WalletPassBuilder offers specialized pass types and tools that can be the right choice for a pass-only project. SuperStack OS is the broader choice when loyalty needs to work alongside customer conversations, campaigns and payments.

The same job, two ways

The job is simple: launch a loyalty card and bring customers back. The difference is what happens beyond the pass itself.

In WalletPassBuilder

  1. Design your loyalty pass using a starter template, personalization, QR codes or barcodes.
  2. Share the pass so customers can save it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
  3. Send a wallet push immediately or schedule it for later.
  4. Buy more pass credits as your program grows beyond the included credits.
  5. Handle customer replies, bookings and payments in other tools.

In SuperStack OS

Connect SuperStack OS to ChatGPT, Claude or Grok once, then describe the loyalty program:

Create a loyalty punch card for my business in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Build a signup page, create a wallet push inviting pass holders back, and set up an AI agent to answer customer questions by SMS and web chat.

The pass, the signup page, the push and the AI agent that answers replies on your connected channels all live in one place. Wallet programs, passes and pushes can be created and sent through the MCP connector, while the built-in CRM keeps the customer context together.

Built MCP first, so your AI assistant is the dashboard

SuperStack OS was engineered around its native MCP app connector from the start. Its 100 native tools let you build and run agents, flows, pages, pipelines, campaigns and wallet programs from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok without opening the SuperStack OS dashboard. Many tools handle more than a dozen actions each, and new users can even create their account inside the connector.

WalletPassBuilder is a specialized wallet pass tool with a REST API, webhooks and a visual automation builder. Those are useful options for connecting a pass program to other software. SuperStack OS takes a different approach: the assistant can operate the loyalty program and the business tools around it in the same system.

One flow builder for automations and conversations

A loyalty program needs more than a reminder on a phone. Customers ask questions, book visits and respond to offers. SuperStack OS combines scripted conversational flows, including buttons, questions, keywords, conditions and waits, with back office automations in one visual builder. AI agents can step in with their own instructions when the conversation needs them.

One AI agent can work across Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X, SMS, email, web chat and phone at the same time. Conversations land in a unified inbox where you can reply directly or respond by email.

Passes share the contact record with the built-in CRM and pipelines. A customer DM can qualify a lead, update a deal stage, send an email, text the owner or trigger an AI phone call. WalletPassBuilder offers visual automations for its pass-focused setup, but it does not include AI chat or voice agents, email campaigns or SMS campaigns.

Who fixes it when it breaks?

A focused pass tool can keep a pass-only project simple. If you also need a CRM, messaging and payments, consider who will maintain the connections between those tools.

  • Can the owner follow the customer journey? In SuperStack OS, the pass, contact record, agents, campaigns and payments live together. With separate tools, decide where your team will look for each part.
  • Who maintains the connections? REST APIs and webhooks are useful, but any custom integration still needs configuration and testing. For WalletPassBuilder hosting and upkeep details: See their site.

SuperStack OS is fully managed and mobile first in the browser, with no separate app to download. You can also operate it from the ChatGPT, Claude or Grok apps you already use. Its agents run on models from OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI, so model improvements can benefit the agents you already have without rebuilding them.

Where WalletPassBuilder is the better choice

WalletPassBuilder has a clear focus and a useful collection of specialized tools. Consider it if:

  • You only want passes. Its no-code builder and starter templates suit restaurants, gyms, events, retail, salons, schools, nonprofits and creators that do not need a full business platform.
  • You need gift cards or event tickets. It supports those pass types alongside memberships, coupons and offers, business cards and loyalty cards.
  • You want prize games. Spin to win and scratch cards are part of its feature set.
  • You need a staff scanner app. WalletPassBuilder includes a free staff Scanner app, plus per-pass stats, team roles and white-label sharing pages.
  • US or EU data residency is a deciding factor. See their site to confirm available residency choices and whether they meet your requirements.

Keep the platform distinction in mind: WalletPassBuilder geofencing and beacons work on Apple Wallet only, not Google Wallet. Choose SuperStack OS when your priority is loyalty points programs and punch cards connected to the CRM, agents, campaigns and payments you use to run the business.

How to switch from WalletPassBuilder to SuperStack OS

  1. Recreate the loyalty program. Describe your points program or punch card to ChatGPT, Claude or Grok through the SuperStack OS MCP connector. Review the program and customer-facing content before launch.
  2. Import your contacts. Bring your customer records and tags into the built-in CRM, keeping messaging consent and contact details accurate.
  3. Invite customers to add the new pass. Share the new signup page and explain how to save the new Apple Wallet or Google Wallet loyalty pass. Do not assume previously saved passes transfer automatically.
  4. Turn on agents and campaigns. Set up wallet pushes, email and SMS follow up, and AI agents that answer questions on your connected channels. Test the customer journey before retiring tools you have replaced.

If you depend on gift cards, tickets, prize games or a staff scanner, evaluate those requirements separately before switching. SuperStack OS wallet programs focus on loyalty points and punch cards, not replacing every specialized pass use case.

Frequently asked questions

Is SuperStack OS a good WalletPassBuilder alternative?

Yes, if you want loyalty passes connected to the rest of your business. SuperStack OS includes Apple Wallet and Google Wallet points programs and punch cards, wallet push notifications and location based alerts, alongside a built-in CRM, AI chat and voice agents, landing pages, email, SMS and payments. WalletPassBuilder is a focused choice if you only need wallet passes or its specialized pass types and tools.

What is WalletPassBuilder best at?

WalletPassBuilder is a no-code Apple Wallet and Google Wallet pass creator for restaurants, gyms, events, retail, salons, schools, nonprofits and creators. It offers loyalty cards, memberships, coupons and offers, gift cards, event tickets and business cards with starter templates. Its tools include pass updates, immediate or scheduled wallet pushes, QR codes and barcodes, personalization, CSV bulk import, a REST API, webhooks, visual automations, prize games, a free staff Scanner app, per-pass stats, team roles and white-label sharing pages. Geofencing and beacons work on Apple Wallet only.

Do I need a CRM to use WalletPassBuilder?

No. WalletPassBuilder is a wallet pass creator, not a CRM, and a CRM is not required to create passes. It does not include a CRM, AI chat or voice agents, a landing page builder, or email and SMS campaigns. Its messaging is limited to wallet push notifications. SuperStack OS brings those business tools and wallet passes into one system.

Can I use WalletPassBuilder and SuperStack OS together?

You can use WalletPassBuilder for passes and SuperStack OS for CRM, agents, campaigns and payments as separate tools. WalletPassBuilder offers a REST API and webhooks, but any connection needs to be configured and tested. Do not assume an automatic sync or that existing passes can be transferred.

How much does SuperStack OS cost compared to WalletPassBuilder?

SuperStack OS is $49 per month for Basic, $79 for PRO and $297 for White-Label. WalletPassBuilder is $49 per month including 1,000 pass credits. Extra packs cost $100 for 1,000 credits, $350 for 5,000, $600 for 10,000 and $1,000 for 25,000. A CRM and email or SMS messaging tools are separate from WalletPassBuilder.
