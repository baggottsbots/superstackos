Why people look for a GoHighLevel alternative

GoHighLevel is one of the most popular platforms for marketing agencies and service businesses. It packs CRM, funnels, websites, email, SMS, calendars, workflows, memberships and reputation tools into a single ecosystem. Yet many small business owners and agency operators find themselves shopping for alternatives.

The three most common reasons users search for a GHL alternative are:

  • Steep learning curve and endless menus: Setting up sub-accounts, custom values, triggers and nested settings requires substantial onboarding time. Simple tasks often demand digging through multiple layers of configuration.
  • Built before AI: GoHighLevel was architected in the pre-AI era. While AI capabilities have been added over time, they often feel bolted onto an older interface and frequently carry extra usage charges.
  • More than a small business needs: Many small businesses find that maintaining a complex agency-grade dashboard creates friction rather than speed. They want an operating system that runs lean and fast.

1. SuperStack OS

SuperStack OS is an all-in-one AI business operating system built for founders, marketers and agencies who want results without complex dashboard management. It combines CRM, AI chat and voice agents that make phone calls, automation flows, landing pages, forms, e-commerce with Stripe checkout, and email and SMS campaigns on a unified contact record.

Unlike older platforms, SuperStack OS is built mobile first and MCP first. Through its native MCP app connector with 100 native tools, you can run your entire business directly from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok using plain English prompts. Everything is fully managed with one unified builder for automations and chat conversations.

Best for: Business owners and agencies who want an AI-native stack they can operate from their phone or favorite AI assistant.

Watch out for: SuperStack OS focuses on clean execution and AI agents rather than deep legacy desktop configurations or sprawling plugin marketplaces.

2. HubSpot

HubSpot is a comprehensive CRM suite divided into specialized Hubs for marketing, sales, customer service, content and operations. It provides free CRM tools that let early-stage teams get started quickly, along with polished contact tracking and deal management.

For mid-sized companies and expanding sales departments, HubSpot delivers exceptional reporting, granular permission controls and dependable pipeline analytics. Its interface is refined and backed by extensive documentation and partner networks.

Best for: Companies with dedicated sales teams that require rigorous activity tracking and deep corporate reporting.

Watch out for: Total investment scales rapidly as you add paid seats, upgrade hub tiers and increase marketing contact counts.

See our full SuperStack OS vs HubSpot comparison →

3. ClickFunnels

ClickFunnels established the modern digital sales funnel model. It specializes in high-converting checkout flows, one-click upsells, order bumps, automated email follow-ups and digital member areas tailored for direct-response marketing.

If your primary focus is selling informational products, books, webinars or training courses, ClickFunnels provides battle-tested templates and checkout funnels engineered to maximize average order value.

Best for: Direct response marketers and creators selling info products through dedicated sales funnels.

Watch out for: It is not a complete local business CRM or AI agent platform, and requires outside tools for advanced customer management and voice calling.

See our full SuperStack OS vs ClickFunnels comparison →

4. Systeme.io

Systeme.io provides an accessible all-in-one software suite designed for entrepreneurs, creators and small business operators. It combines sales funnels, email marketing broadcasts, online course hosting and affiliate management into a single platform.

With an available free tier and straightforward setup, it offers one of the lowest barriers to entry in the marketing automation space, making it a common choice for beginners launching their first digital offers.

Best for: Solopreneurs and new businesses on a tight budget who need essential funnels and email lists without high software overhead.

Watch out for: Lacks native AI voice calling agents, deep conversational messaging flows and agency white label capabilities.

See our full SuperStack OS vs Systeme.io comparison →

5. ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign is a leader in marketing automation, customer experience automation and email marketing. Its visual automation builder allows teams to craft sophisticated branching email sequences triggered by user behavior, website visits and engagement scores.

The platform also includes a built-in sales CRM with lead scoring and pipeline management, giving marketing and sales teams cohesive visibility into prospect interactions across their customer journey.

Best for: Businesses focused heavily on segmented email marketing, behavior-driven triggers and email deliverability.

Watch out for: It is primarily an email and CRM engine, requiring outside software for full website hosting, phone agents and complete agency sub-account resale.

See our full SuperStack OS vs ActiveCampaign comparison →

6. Kajabi

Kajabi is built specifically for knowledge commerce, online educators, coaches and community creators. It brings together course hosting, membership portals, community forums, podcasts, coaching programs and checkout pages into one polished environment.

Because it specializes in student and member experiences, Kajabi provides rich content delivery, video streaming, quizzes and member progress tracking out of the box without requiring third-party plugins.

Best for: Creators and coaches whose core business revolves around selling premium online courses and community memberships.

Watch out for: Less suitable as a general-purpose CRM or agency platform for local service businesses needing SMS dispatch, booking and AI phone agents.

For agencies: white label alternatives to GHL

GoHighLevel became famous largely because of its white label SaaS mode, allowing marketing agencies to rebrand the software and sell it as their own proprietary tool. For agencies seeking a modern alternative with AI at the center, SuperStack OS offers a dedicated White-Label tier.

The SuperStack OS White-Label plan is $297 per month and includes:

  • Unlimited client sub-accounts with separate customer records and databases
  • Your own custom domain and branding
  • Full freedom over your own pricing and client billing
  • A white labeled MCP server so your clients can run their sub-accounts from ChatGPT, Claude and Grok

This enables agencies to provide an AI-native operating system to their clients without the operational complexity of managing legacy node graphs or multi-layered desktop settings.

When you should stay on GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel remains a powerhouse platform, and switching software carries real transition costs. You should stay on GHL if:

  • Your team is already fully trained: If your staff knows GHL inside and out, the learning curve is already behind you.
  • You rely heavily on snapshots: GHL snapshots allow you to clone entire account setups in seconds, which is valuable if your agency workflow relies on standardized template drops.
  • You depend on the GHL app marketplace: If you use specialized third-party marketplace apps or community extensions built specifically for GHL.
  • You actively use its membership and reputation management modules: If built-in review request widgets and GHL membership portals are central to your current client retainers.
