What is an MCP business platform?

Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Grok interact directly with business software. Instead of copying and pasting information between tabs, your AI assistant can execute actions inside connected software using structured tools.

An MCP business platform provides the underlying software infrastructure that the AI assistant controls. Through a native MCP connector, your AI assistant becomes your operational dashboard, letting you run campaigns, manage contacts, build landing pages, deploy voice agents, and automate workflows simply by describing what you want in plain English.

How to judge an MCP connector

Not all MCP connections are built equal. When evaluating platforms that connect to ChatGPT, Claude or Grok, consider these five key criteria:

  • Depth of tool coverage: How many native tools are exposed, and do they cover the entire product or just a single feature?
  • Creation vs execution: Can your AI assistant build new assets (pages, agents, pipelines) or can it only trigger existing workflows?
  • Integrated business backend: Does the platform include a full CRM, voice platform, and marketing tools behind the connector, or is it just a middleware bridge?
  • Zero-dashboard onboarding: Can new users sign up and configure systems directly through the AI assistant conversation without opening a web dashboard?
  • Agency white-labeling: Can agencies deliver white labeled MCP servers to clients under their own brand and custom domain?

2. Zapier

Zapier offers an MCP product that lets AI assistants run actions in the apps you have connected to Zapier. According to their public documentation, it allows AI models to trigger actions across connected third-party tools, bridging AI assistants with thousands of web apps.

Because Zapier focuses on app integration, its MCP capabilities center around invoking existing Zaps and triggering external third-party actions rather than hosting an integrated CRM or native landing page builder.

Best for: Teams with existing Zapier workflows who want AI assistants to execute actions across diverse SaaS applications.

Watch out for: Zapier is an integration bridge rather than an all-in-one business operating system with built-in CRM or voice agents.

3. Make

Make offers an MCP server that lets AI assistants run your scenarios. According to their public documentation, your AI assistant can execute visual workflows created in Make, passing variables and triggering complex multi-branch integrations.

Make excels at complex visual data mapping between third-party services. Through MCP, an AI assistant can activate these scenarios, though users still design and maintain the underlying scenarios inside Make's visual canvas editor.

Best for: Technical power users and developers who rely on Make for advanced data transformation and workflow routing.

Watch out for: Building and debugging scenario graphs requires technical logic, and Make does not include a native CRM or phone agent platform.

4. n8n

n8n offers MCP nodes that you configure per workflow. According to their public documentation, technical teams can connect AI models directly to specific n8n node executions, combining self-hosted workflow automation with AI capabilities.

For teams requiring strict data privacy or local hosting, n8n provides source-available workflow building. The MCP node setup requires technical configuration for each workflow and node graph.

Best for: Engineers and IT teams who need self-hosted workflow automation with custom code support.

Watch out for: n8n requires technical knowledge to configure nodes and maintain hosting infrastructure, with no native marketing or CRM tools included.

5. GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel offers an MCP server for its CRM. According to their public documentation, it allows AI assistants to interact with client records, sales pipelines, and agency sub-accounts.

For marketing agencies managing multiple local business sub-accounts, GoHighLevel's MCP connector brings conversational access to client CRM data and snapshots.

Best for: Traditional marketing agencies already heavily invested in the GoHighLevel ecosystem.

Watch out for: The interface and setup remain complex, requiring desktop management for deep workflow and page configurations.

6. HubSpot

HubSpot offers connectors that let AI assistants work with CRM data. According to their public documentation, AI models can read and update CRM contacts, deals, and marketing records.

HubSpot's MCP integration serves mid-market and enterprise sales teams who want their AI assistants to query account status, summarize contacts, and update sales deals directly from chat.

Best for: Corporate sales and marketing teams storing primary customer relationships inside HubSpot.

Watch out for: HubSpot is primarily a CRM and inbound marketing suite rather than an AI agent execution platform with built-in voice calling or plain English app creation.

Frequently asked questions

What is MCP in simple terms?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Grok interact directly with outside software. Instead of just answering questions with text, an AI assistant with an MCP connection can read data, run actions, and build workflows in connected platforms.

Which business platforms work with ChatGPT and Claude through MCP?

Platforms like SuperStack OS, Zapier, Make, n8n, GoHighLevel and HubSpot offer MCP connectors or nodes. SuperStack OS is built MCP first with 100 native tools covering the entire business operating system, while other platforms offer MCP access to their specific automation or CRM capabilities.

Do I need to be technical to use MCP?

No. Once connected inside ChatGPT, Claude or Grok, you speak to your AI assistant in plain English. Platforms built MCP first like SuperStack OS allow non-technical business owners to create landing pages, flows, CRM pipelines and campaigns without writing code or logging into a complex dashboard.

Is MCP safe for my business data?

You authorize the connection and approve what the assistant can do; review each vendor's security documentation.

Can agencies offer MCP under their own brand?

Yes, on SuperStack OS White-Label plan ($297 per month), agencies get a white labeled MCP server along with custom domain, branding, and unlimited client sub-accounts.
