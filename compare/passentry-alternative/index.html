Wallet passes and customer loyalty

SuperStack OS vs PassEntry: wallet passes plus the whole business

By SuperStack OS Team•Last verified November 2026

The short answer

PassEntry is a focused wallet pass platform for creating and managing Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes, including ticketing, transit, loyalty and membership. SuperStack OS has wallet passes built into a complete AI business operating system. Loyalty programs, wallet pushes and location based alerts sit alongside your CRM, AI agents, campaigns and payments, all run from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok.

One piece of the puzzle vs the whole puzzle

A wallet pass is one tool, not a business. PassEntry creates and manages the pass with real-time updates, notifications and analytics. When you need a CRM record, a wider email campaign or a connected workflow, you turn to your CRM, your email tool and integrations such as Zapier.

SuperStack OS issues the loyalty pass and already has the customer, the conversation, the follow up and the payment in the same place on one flat monthly plan. Passes sit on the same contact record as your CRM, AI agents, campaigns and payments. The surrounding tools are included:

  • Apple Wallet and Google Wallet points programs and punch cards, wallet push notifications and location based alerts, with no separate customer app to download
  • AI chat and voice agents on Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X, SMS, email, web chat and phone, with one unified inbox
  • Built-in CRM with pipelines, tags and custom fields
  • Automation flows and conversational flows in one builder
  • An AI Coding Agent that builds full multi-page websites, landing pages, interactive quizzes and games, plus forms and a web shop with Stripe checkout
  • Email and SMS broadcasts, post scheduling for Facebook, Instagram and X, and comment automation for Instagram comments, Facebook comments and X mentions
  • AI content generation for images, video, voice and music
  • A white label option so agencies can sell it as their own platform

A fair note on fit. PassEntry offers specialized pass types and integrations that matter when passes are the product. SuperStack OS is the stronger fit when loyalty is one part of a business that also needs conversations, follow up, sales and payments.

The same job, two ways

The job: launch a loyalty card and bring customers back. Both approaches can put a loyalty pass in a customer's wallet. The difference is where the rest of the work happens.

In PassEntry

  1. Build the pass template with the no-code template builder. CSV batch uploads are also available.
  2. Connect your CRM or Zapier for the customer data and workflows around the pass.
  3. Distribute the pass by SMS, email or QR code.
  4. Send a wallet push notification or use a geo-triggered notification.
  5. Handle customer replies, bookings and payments in your other tools.

In SuperStack OS

Connect SuperStack OS to ChatGPT, Claude or Grok once, then describe the loyalty program:

Create a loyalty punch card for Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, build a signup page, prepare a wallet push inviting customers back, and create an AI agent to answer loyalty questions through web chat and SMS.

The pass, the signup page, the push and the AI agent that answers customer replies through your messaging channels all live in one place. Wallet programs, passes and pushes can be created and sent through the MCP connector, while the CRM keeps the customer context alongside campaigns and payments.

Built MCP first, so your AI assistant is the dashboard

SuperStack OS was engineered around its native MCP app connector from the start. Its 100 native tools let you run the business from ChatGPT, Claude or Grok without opening the SuperStack OS dashboard. Many tools handle more than a dozen actions each, and new users can even create their account inside the connector.

That includes creating wallet programs and passes and sending wallet pushes by prompt, alongside agents, pages, pipelines, campaigns and workflows. You describe the business outcome instead of stitching separate tools together.

PassEntry is a specialized wallet pass tool with a REST API and public documentation. That is a useful foundation for developer teams building pass functionality into an existing stack. Its listed integrations include Zapier, HubSpot CRM, Salesforce, Shopify, Stripe and Ticketmaster. For assistant connectivity beyond those listed capabilities, see their site.

One flow builder for automations and conversations

A loyalty program should not stop at the wallet notification. SuperStack OS combines back office automations and scripted conversational flows in one visual builder, including buttons, questions, keywords, conditions and waits. Real AI agents can step in with their own instructions, so structured steps and conversational AI work together.

One AI agent can run on Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, X (Twitter), SMS, email, web chat and phone calls at the same time. Conversations arrive in a unified inbox where you can reply directly or respond by email.

When a customer asks about a loyalty offer through those channels, the built-in CRM and pipelines keep the conversation connected to the contact. A DM can qualify a lead, update a deal stage, send an email, text the owner or trigger an AI phone call. Lead qualification and booking stay in the same system as the loyalty program.

Who fixes it when it breaks?

PassEntry's dedicated pass tools can fit well into an established stack. The operational question is who owns the connections between the pass program, CRM, messaging and payment tools.

  • Can the owner follow the customer journey? Map where the pass is created, where the contact lives and where a customer's reply is handled.
  • Who maintains the connections? External integrations still need configuration, permissions and someone responsible for troubleshooting them.

SuperStack OS is fully managed and mobile first in the browser, with no separate app to download. The wallet program and the business tools share one platform. Its agents run on models from OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI, so model improvements benefit the agents you already have without rebuilding them.

Where PassEntry is the better choice

PassEntry deserves credit for its dedicated wallet pass focus. It is worth choosing or evaluating first when:

  • You need ticketing, transit or boarding passes. PassEntry lists these pass types. They are not the focus of SuperStack OS.
  • You are evaluating high-volume enterprise pass programs. Its CSV batch uploads, real-time updates, analytics dashboard and REST API make it a relevant specialist to assess. Confirm volume limits, service terms and pricing directly.
  • Your developers want a dedicated pass API. PassEntry provides a REST API with public documentation for teams integrating passes into their own systems.
  • Your company is standardized on Salesforce, HubSpot or Ticketmaster. PassEntry lists integrations with those platforms, letting you evaluate passes within the stack you already use.

Choose SuperStack OS when you want more than a pass: a loyalty program connected to the CRM, conversations, campaigns and payments, operated by a non-technical owner from the AI assistant already on their phone.

How to switch from PassEntry to SuperStack OS

  1. Recreate the loyalty program. Describe your points program or punch card to ChatGPT, Claude or Grok through the SuperStack OS MCP connector. Check the program details before launch.
  2. Import your contacts. Bring customer contacts and tags into the built-in CRM, retaining the permissions needed for your campaigns.
  3. Invite customers to add the new pass. Explain the change and ask customers to add their new Apple Wallet or Google Wallet loyalty pass. Do not assume an existing PassEntry pass transfers automatically.
  4. Turn on agents and campaigns. Set up wallet pushes, location based alerts, email and SMS follow up, and AI agents to answer questions through your connected channels.
  5. Test before retiring the old setup. Verify the customer journey, then turn off only what you have replaced. SuperStack OS flows accept incoming webhooks for workflows you choose to keep connected.

Frequently asked questions

Is SuperStack OS a good PassEntry alternative?

Yes, for a business that wants loyalty passes plus the tools around them. SuperStack OS includes Apple Wallet and Google Wallet points programs and punch cards, wallet push notifications and location based alerts, alongside a built-in CRM, AI agents, campaigns and payments. Customers do not need a separate app. PassEntry is the more specialized choice for ticketing, transit and boarding passes.

What is PassEntry best at?

PassEntry specializes in creating and managing Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes for ticketing, transit, boarding passes, loyalty and membership. Its listed features include real-time updates, push and geo-triggered notifications, SMS, email and QR code distribution, analytics, a no-code template builder, CSV batch uploads and a REST API with public documentation.

Do I need a CRM to use PassEntry?

A separate CRM is not stated as a requirement for creating passes. PassEntry does not have a built-in CRM and connects to external CRMs such as HubSpot and Salesforce. If you need CRM contact records and pipelines alongside your passes, use an external CRM or choose SuperStack OS, where they are built in.

Can I use PassEntry and SuperStack OS together?

You can use PassEntry for specialized pass programs and SuperStack OS for CRM, agents, campaigns and payments. PassEntry lists a REST API and Zapier integration, but a direct SuperStack OS integration is not established here. Check the available integrations and API documentation before planning any data synchronization.

How much does SuperStack OS cost compared to PassEntry?

SuperStack OS costs $49 per month for Basic, $79 for PRO and $297 for White-Label. Wallet passes are included in PRO and White-Label. PassEntry states no prices on its homepage and offers a free trial and demo booking. See current pricing on its site, and budget separately for any external CRM and messaging tools.
